🛡️ About TrustLens AI

TrustLens AI is an AI-based scam detection and digital fraud awareness platform.

🤖 What does TrustLens AI do?

It analyzes suspicious messages using machine learning and predicts whether the message resembles a scam or a legitimate message.

🎯 Our Goal

Our goal is to make people more aware of digital scams and help them recognize suspicious messages before taking action.

🌐 Multilingual Awareness

The project is designed with multilingual digital safety awareness in mind, including English, Hindi and Marathi.
